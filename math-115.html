MATH 115: College Algebra

A study of algebraic equations, inequalities, graphs, and functions — including linear, quadratic, absolute value, rational, radical, polynomial, exponential, and logarithmic functions. Prerequisite: ACT Math 21 or higher, SAT Math 530 or higher, or MATH 108 with a "C" or higher.

Materials below are contributed by faculty teaching MATH 115 and shared department-wide for use in online sections. Originally developed by Pai Song; faculty using these materials in their own sections should adapt pacing and dates to their syllabus. Notes link to PDFs hosted alongside this site.
